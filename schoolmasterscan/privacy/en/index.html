1. Data controller and contact details

SCHOOLMASTERSCAN is developed and published by Francesco Zanaglio, an independent developer operating under the name FZO.

For privacy information or requests, please write to:

fzo.developer@gmail.com

Official website: https://fzodeveloper-eng.github.io/

2. General principles

SCHOOLMASTERSCAN is an Android application for capturing or importing document images, recognizing their text through optical character recognition (OCR), applying corrections and graphic annotations, and saving or sharing the result.

The app is designed to process images, recognized text and corrections locally. It does not require an FZO account and does not use FZO servers to receive or store documents. FZO has no remote or centralized archive of processed documents.

Some functions use Google components and Android services that may process technical information under their respective privacy policies, as described below.

3. Documents and data processed on the device

Depending on the functions selected by the user, the app may locally process:

  • images captured with the camera or scanner and images selected through the Android picker;
  • OCR-recognized text, including words, lines and their positions;
  • selections, marks, redactions, colors and text entered by the user;
  • preferences relating, among other things, to language, theme, colors, fonts, display and acquisition method;
  • local information about acceptance of the Terms of Use, developer mode, Premium access and the reviewer DEMO.

Documents selected by the user may contain personal data, data relating to children or other third parties, confidential information and, depending on their content, special categories of personal data.

The app's FZO code does not automatically send images, OCR text, corrections or documents to FZO. The app does not create a database or permanent history of editable documents.

4. Capture, scanner and OCR

Direct camera capture creates a temporary image in the app's private cache. Gallery selection uses the Android content picker and concerns only the image chosen by the user.

The Scanner function uses Google Play services Document Scanner, configured for one page and JPEG output. Google supplies the interface, scanning logic and required models through Play services; according to Google's documentation, the scanning flow operates on the device.

Text recognition uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition. Images and OCR results are processed on the device and are not sent to Google servers to perform recognition.

ML Kit nevertheless collects technical data for diagnostics and usage analytics, including device and app information, per-installation technical identifiers, performance metrics such as latency, API configuration and image format or resolution, input and output size, feature version, event types and error codes. Google states that this data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is not transferred by ML Kit to third parties. Components may also communicate with Google for downloads, updates, fixes and compatibility checks.

Further information:

  • https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure
  • https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/vision/doc-scanner/android
  • https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Saving and sharing

When the user selects Save, the app produces one JPEG of the processed document and inserts it into the device's shared image collection through Android MediaStore, normally in Pictures/SCHOOLMASTERSCAN. The file remains in the gallery until the user or a service used by the user deletes it; uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete it.

When the user selects Share, the app instead produces a temporary JPEG in its private cache, without adding an image to the gallery. Android grants read access to the file to the recipient app selected by the user. Previous sharing files in the cache folder are removed when a new share is prepared; the cache may also be deleted by the system, by clearing app data or by uninstalling the app.

Recipient applications and people may independently retain or process the copies they receive. FZO does not control that subsequent processing.

6. Preferences, Android backup and device transfer

Settings and other eligible app states are stored locally in the app's preferences. The Android configuration allows cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of eligible preferences, depending on the user's device, account settings and Android services.

This backup is not a document archive: it does not include captured or imported images, OCR text, corrections, temporary cache JPEGs or JPEGs saved in the gallery as the app's private data.

Preferences dedicated to the reviewer DEMO and the local Premium entitlement state are expressly excluded from both cloud backup and device transfer. A Premium purchase is verified and restored by querying Google Play.

JPEGs saved in the gallery may separately be covered by photo backup or synchronization services enabled by the user; those services operate independently of SCHOOLMASTERSCAN's app-data backup.

7. Premium features, Google Play Billing and reviewer DEMO

SCHOOLMASTERSCAN integrates Google Play Billing 9.1.0. Premium features are offered through the one-time, non-consumable purchase sms_premium_unlock, with no recurring subscription.

Google Play manages presentation of the product and price, the purchase, payment, confirmation and information associated with the Google account. The app communicates with Google Play to query the product, start the purchase, verify its status, acknowledge it and restore a previously purchased Premium entitlement. FZO does not receive complete payment-card details and does not directly process financial transactions.

The app stores locally only an enabled state needed to make Premium functions available; FZO code does not locally store the purchase token. Google Play remains the authoritative source for restoring the purchase.

The Google Play reviewer DEMO is a local mode separate from the purchase. The entered code is checked on the device by comparing its cryptographic digest and does not start a payment or alter purchase ownership. DEMO status is kept in a separate local preference, can be disabled separately, and is excluded from backup and device transfer.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. Diagnostics and promotional messages

SCHOOLMASTERSCAN does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, FZO profiling systems, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or an FZO automatic telemetry collection service.

A local diagnostics function, available after developer mode is enabled, keeps a limited number of technical events in memory. The report may include the app and Android versions, API level, device manufacturer and model, available memory and storage, acquisition method, MIME type when available, image dimensions, operation timing and outcomes, word and line counts, and error classes. It is not designed to include images, OCR text, correction text, document paths, URIs or names.

The report is not automatically transmitted to FZO. It leaves the app only if the user chooses to copy it to the clipboard or share it through a recipient app.

The app may display self-promotional messages about other FZO applications. Promotional content is embedded in the app; opening Google Play or a browser requires user action, and subsequent processing depends on the service opened.

9. Purposes and legal bases

Processing connected with the app is intended to enable document capture, recognition, editing, saving and sharing; store preferences and app states; manage and restore Premium features; provide local DEMO access to reviewers; and answer communications sent to FZO.

Where Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) applies, processing carried out under FZO's responsibility and necessary to provide functions requested by the user is based, where relevant, on performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b)).

Communications voluntarily sent to FZO are processed to answer requests and may be based, as appropriate, on performance of the contract or the legitimate interest in handling relevant communications (Article 6(1)(f)). Legal obligations may justify processing under Article 6(1)(c); establishing, exercising or defending legal claims may be based on legitimate interests within the limits allowed by law.

Acceptance of the Terms of Use does not automatically constitute consent to personal-data processing. Processing carried out independently by Google and other services is described in their respective privacy policies.

10. Retention and deletion

Images and editor data normally remain in memory or cache for as long as needed to use the function. SCHOOLMASTERSCAN does not maintain a permanent archive of editable documents.

Local preferences remain until they are changed, reset or deleted. Temporary cache files may be replaced or deleted by the app or system, by clearing app data, or by uninstalling the app.

Clearing app data or uninstalling the app normally removes local private data, but does not necessarily delete:

  • JPEGs saved in the gallery;
  • copies shared with other applications or people;
  • copies retained by photo backup or synchronization services;
  • eligible preferences already retained by the Android backup service, in accordance with its rules;
  • purchase information retained by Google Play.

Communications sent directly to FZO are retained for as long as necessary to handle the request and any related legal obligations or needs to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

11. Recipients, external providers and transfers

FZO does not operate a centralized archive of users' documents. Depending on the functions used, information may be processed or received by:

  • Google, through Android, Google Play services, ML Kit and Google Play Billing;
  • the provider of the Android backup or transfer service configured by the user;
  • applications and people selected by the user as sharing recipients;
  • FZO, only for information voluntarily sent, for example by email.

The roles and responsibilities of external providers depend on the individual service and processing operation. For Google's processing and any international transfers and related safeguards, please refer to the provider's privacy policies and documentation.

12. Children and third-party content

SCHOOLMASTERSCAN is a general document capture and correction tool and is not specifically directed at children.

Processed documents may contain data relating to children or other third parties. The user is responsible for having the rights, permissions and other necessary grounds to capture, edit, save and share such content.

The app does not ask for the user's age and does not use documents to identify or profile the people represented in them.

13. Data-subject rights

Where provided by the GDPR, data subjects may exercise rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, objection and portability, subject to the applicable conditions.

Requests concerning personal data that may be processed directly by FZO can be sent to:

fzo.developer@gmail.com

Content stored only on the device can be managed directly by the user. FZO has no remote access to that content and cannot delete copies retained by Google, backup services, recipient applications or other people.

Data subjects may lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. In Italy: Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, https://www.garanteprivacy.it/.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application, the services used or applicable law. The updated version will be published on the official FZO website with the last-updated date. Users will be informed through appropriate means if changes are material.
